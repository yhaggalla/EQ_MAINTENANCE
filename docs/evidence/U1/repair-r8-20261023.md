## 2 Changes

- **U1-C28 TV mode** (`js/views-fleet.js`, `js/app.js`, `css/eq-fleet.css`): `#/eq/fleet?tv=1`; `body.tv` now also for EQ Fleet when the role has `eq.fleet.view`; top bar hidden; visible "EQ Fleet" title; "Updated hh:mm Asia/Colombo - refreshes every 60 s" (same 60 s poll and same re-render on data changes); ZMMS freshness; KPI tiles; groups with large cards that are not links; footer with status meaning and blink legend. Named `<g data-part>` groups, no images, references or gradients. Used on cards, group headers and the Active equipment KPI tile. D-164.

D-165.
- **U1-C31 restyle** (new `css/eq-fleet.css`, linked from `index.html`): new `--pa-*` / `--blink-*` tokens, every rule scoped to `.fleet-page.pa` or `body[data-page="eq-fleet"]` (set by `app.js`); KPI tile row (Active with illustrations, Ready for OPS share donut, four status filter tiles); white rounded cards and filter panel; U1-C02 density kept.
