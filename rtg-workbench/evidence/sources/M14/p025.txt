2.4.3 Trolley frame 
Trolley frame is one of the main structure of the RTG. It directly bears the lifting load and transfer the load 
to the gantry frame through the wheels. The trolley frame is composed of two I-shaped girders and two end 
beams. The frame will be mounted with wheels, driving device, hoisting device, operator cab, and other 
auxiliary equipments to be a complete trolley.

The hoist mechanism is shown as Fig 2-10. High speed and low speed shafts of the reducer are on the same 
level. The upper and lower housings of the reducer are split from the center line of the gears and subdivision 
surface is sealed with sealant. At the bottom of the low speed shaft frame is provided with an oil discharge valve 
and oil level indicator, a scale is arranged on indicator for easier observation of lubrication oil. The handhole 
cover in the high-speed shaft frame is convenient for cleaning the inner cavity of reducer. The cover is equipped 
with air breather, and observation hole. The main hoist drum is made of steel plate and of welded construction. 
The pitch diameter of drum is above 30 times of the nominal diameter of wire rope. The drum has four sets of 
grooves, One side of the drum is connected to the reducer by a drum coupling, another side of the drum is sitting 
on the trolley frame through the bearing seat.
