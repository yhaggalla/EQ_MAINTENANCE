5.4.4 Inspection content 
To find the wire rope wear and break as early as 
possible, schedule should be made according to it actual 
situation. A whole inspection procedure should include 
the follows: 
1) 
Inspect wire rope diameter to see if it is 
decreasing, and how about the decrease rate. 
2) 
If there is any corrosion, and how about the 
lubrication 
3) 
The exterior and interior wear situation. 
4) 
If there is any visible break, and the property 
of the break. Wire rope must be changed if the 
break reaches specified degree. 
5) 
If there is any phenomenon of crushing, 
flattening, bird cage flattening, kinking, or 
other distortion, kinking wire rope and other 
kinds of severe deformation wire rope cannot 
be repaired and used. 
6) 
Inspect the components which are connected or 
contacted with the wire rope, broken joint must 
be changed immediately; the surface situation 
of the sheave and drum grooves, if they are 
reach the changeable degree. 
The result of the inspection should be recorded in 
the work diary or special log. 
 
The inspection result should 
be recorded for following. 
Refer to ISO 4309:2004(E) Annex B which lists the 
recommend format for single inspection and working 
record. The working record can be used to predict the 
wire rope lifetime and do more exact inspection period, it 
also can be used to analyze and take measures which can 
increase the lifetime of the wire rope.  
5.4.5 Discard criteria 
Wire rope’s safety usage is qualified by the 
following criteria: 
1) 
Broken wires: property and quantity, wire 
break at the joint position, concentrate wire 
break and the increase rate of the broken wires. 
Usually wire break is the main damage form in 
RTG hoist, trolley, tray trolley. 
2) 
Wire strand breakage 
3) 
Reduced wire rope diameter, and the wire 
core’s degeneration. 
4) 
Weakening of the elasticity 
5) 
Exterior and interior wear 
6) 
Distortion: waviness, cage flatten, wire core or 
wire strand protrusion or distortion, wire 
protrusion, local increase in wire rope diameter, 
flattening, kinking, bending. 
7) 
Damage cause by electric arc or heating 
8) 
Rate of permanency elongation 
Not only consider the each aspect impacts 
individually, but also consider the comprehensive impacts 
to judge if the wire rope reaches the discard criteria.   
The discard criteria can be found in GB/T5972-2006, 
ISO 4309:2004（E），DIN15020, DIN3088 and etc. The 
local criteria shall be taken priority of those criteria. Due 
to a variety of factors, there is no precise rule deciding 
when the wire rope must be discarded. According to the 
judgment of qualified personnel, it may be allowed to use 
to the end of the shift even if the wire rope meet one of
