Next is the procedure of realign the new drum coupling. 
Newly bought coupling should be anti-corrosive treated, 
sealed well. 
 
Figure4-27 folded drum coupling 
For the following cleaning and installation, first take down 
the inner teeth flange, outer teeth hub and abrasion 
indicator.  
 
Figure4-28 step15 
Clean the inner teeth flange and outer teeth hub 
thoroughly. 
 
Figure4-29 step16 
Heat the outer teeth hub in electric furnace, if no choice, 
oil bath also be accepted. 
 
Figure4-30 step17 
After preparations, start installation, first insert the 
abrasion indicator. 
 
Figure4-31 step18 
Insert outer teeth hub, for fear the hub cooling down, the 
hub should be installed ASAP, and to the right position, up 
to shaft shoulder of reducer.
