At the end of the drum, two switches are connectted to the drum shaft, one is overspeed protection switch,

2.4.4.2 Wire rope reeving system 
Main hoist device use the wire rope to lifting and lowering the spreader.  
 
 
 
 
Figure 2-12 8 ropes reeving system  
 
8 ropes reeving system  
As shown in Fig 2-14, the auxiliary antisway wire ropes are directly replaced by hoist wire ropes. The force 
of hoist rope is far bigger than auxiliary antisway wire rope force. 8 ropes reeving system use 12 sheaves to 
enlarge the dip angle of the wire ropes. One end of the ropes are clamped on the drum, another end of the ropes 
go round the sheaves on the trolley, and then two ropes a group connected  into midpoint of headblock’s four 
edges though adjusting turnbuckle, and form four equal isosceles triangles. Because of the eight wire ropes are 
diagonal connect with the headblock, their horizontal force prevent the spreader sway in both gantry and trolley 
direction. So the 8 ropes reeving system don’t need additional anti-sway device.  
 
Torque of the hoist brake 3680Nm!
