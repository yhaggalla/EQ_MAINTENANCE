7.1.1 routine inspection 
1) 
Frequently inspect the welded and related part of the main structure member, especially the supporting part, 
once found the abnormal thing, the structure need to be repaired. 
2) 
If there is obviously concave- convex phenomenon on the plate of the box structure, crane need to be shut 
down to find out the cause and only can be restart after eliminating the hidden danger. 
3) 
Frequently check the bolts of the trolley rail clips, or welded rail’s welding line and if the bumper support 
at the girder end has been impacted to deformation. 
4) 
Regular check the deformation and welding line of the trolley frame. Pay attention to the working 
condition of the trolley wheel axle. Once found the abnormal status, measures should be taken. 
5) 
Pay attention to the tightness of the bolts. 
6) 
Broken paint part of the structure should be removed the rust instantly and repaint. 
 
7.1.2  periodic annual inspection 
1) 
Fatigue cracks may occur after the metal structure has been used for several years. Except the routine 
inspection, the structure also needs to do the periodic annual inspection. 
2) 
Interval of the periodic annual inspection is 3~5 years. 
3) 
The critical inspection points are those with variation stress and concentration stress points. 
4) 
Inspection method: VT, MT, UT 
5) 
Record the text, and solve the problem when it come out. 
 
7.1.3 metal structure repair 
Repair methods should be taken, when flaw or crack turn out. Repair methods should according to the situation 
of the specific location. Repairmen of the structure is very complicated, consumers cannot judge it by themselves. 
Connecting with ZPMC after- sales service department and design department will help consumers to get the correct 
methods.
