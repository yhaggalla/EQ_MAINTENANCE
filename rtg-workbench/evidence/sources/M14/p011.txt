Chapter 2 Brief introduction of RTG 
 
Summary： 
 
This chapter is a starting point. It aims at providing the fundamental 
knowledge about the construction, functions, performances and features 
of the crane for the operators and maintenance personnel in order that 
they can conduct the operating and maintenance in proper, safe and 
efficient way. 
 
This chapter will describe the RTG’s the general features, including 
structure constructions and the compositions, layout, technical 
parameters, functions, characteristics of the mechanisms. The three main 
mechanisms, the Main Hoist, Trolley Drive and Gantry Travel, will be the 
primary part and detailed. Furthermore, the auxiliary components of the 
crane will be included as well.
