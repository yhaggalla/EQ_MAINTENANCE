Inspection of gantry travel tires 
The gantry travel is indispensable for operation by 
this machine. Therefore, it is important to maintain the 
travel wheels in normal state all times. The following 
passages describe the inspection procedure of the wheel. 
The gantry tires is easy influenced by foreign hard 
things, so inspection of tires need be careful. Include 
tires and steel ring. Ref to Figure3-35 and 3-36 
The following items should be checked during the 
daily, monthly, and annual periodical inspections 
1) 
Air pressure. The normal air pressure is 
10kg/m2. the air pressure should between 
9.6kg/m2 10.4kg/m2。 
2) 
Check the surface of tires,and need to clean 
foreign thing,if find damage,and the depth of 
damage is more than 10mm,should more 
attention to check air leakage.if find air 
leakage,change the tires. 
3) 
Gantry Acceleration will strike the tire, 
especially turning tires without jack up device 
will wear the tires,if find air leakage,change 
the tires.

3.6.8 
Inspection of trolley travel wheel 
The trolley travel are indispensable for operation 
by this machine. Therefore, it is important to maintain 
the travel wheels and rail in normal state all times. The 
following passages describe the inspection procedure of 
the wheel. 
The following items should be checked during the daily, 
monthly, and annual periodical inspections. 
(1)  Wear in treads and flanges 
(2)  No flanges riding on rail 
(3)  During traveling, both sides moving smoothly in 
parallel 
(4)  Abnormal noise or vibration 
(5)  Wear in wheels, bearings, etc., developing evenly 
for both legs.
