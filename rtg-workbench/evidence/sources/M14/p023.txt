2.4.2 Gantry traveling mechanism 
Gantry traveling mechanism has two models, 8 wheels and 16 wheels. It consists of driving device, steering 
device, carriage, slewing bearing, equalizer beam and tubeless tyres. 8 wheels model divide into two wheels 
diagonal driving and four wheels four corners driving. 16 wheels model are all four corners driving, half of the 
wheels are drive wheels.  
The gantry usually working along the yard in straight line, and also the gantry can turn 90° to transfer the 
stacking yard.  
As an option, RTG can rotate around the vertical center line as the arbitrary angle 0-360 °(pivot function), 
to change orientation of operator cab.  
 
2.4.2.1 Eight Wheels Gantry traveling mechanism 
8 wheels traveling mechanism include 8 tubeless tyres. Each of the two tyres together with the frame, 
slewing bearings, hydraulic or electric steering device, equalizer beam form four sets of bogies (driving and 
driven), below the four legs.  
The equalizer beam connects the sill beam by equalizer pin. Two sets of bogies on the diagonal side of 
gantry frame are the drive bogie. Outside of the tyre is drive wheel, equipped with independent driving 
mechanism. It consists of motor, reducer, sprockets and double strand bushroller chain.  
Tyre size 21.00-35(40 Ply), rim model is 35155022/35155023. Tyres are equipped with protective railing. 
Four outside of the corner frame equipped with anti-collision device.  
This project has four sets of drive wheels.
