3.4 
Inspection of alignment 
For rotary components such as couplings, inspection 
of alignment should be completed periodically. The 
alignment of couplings has great influence on the 
mechanism running and life. Therefore, the coupling 
alignment should be checked periodically. In the first year 
that the crane is brought into service, the inspection cycle 
should be shorter, e.g., once per three months. After the 
mechanism get stable in the service, the inspection cycle 
can be lengthened suitably. This inspection mainly 
contrapose the motor coupling and drum coupling of main 
hoist, and trolley travel drive and the motor coupling of 
gantry travel drive. The standard of alignment is 
determined by the request of motor’s alignment and the 
style of coupling. Figure 3-3 shows the main check items 
of coupling’s alignment. Table 3-7 shows the standard’s 
detail of coupling’s alignment.
