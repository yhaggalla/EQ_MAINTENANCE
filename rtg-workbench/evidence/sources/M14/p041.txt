3.1. General 
Crane Maintenance is composed of Inspection, 
Routine Maintenance and Repair. This Chapter 
basically expands upon the operating maintenance and 
routine inspection of the crane. 
The inspection and routine maintenance is used to 
check whether the crane is in a safe state and to place the 
crane into a condition where a high confidence of reliable 
operation can be developed. The main work of the 
inspection and routine maintenance includes cleanliness, 
lubrication, fastening and connections, spot-check and 
periodic inspection. 
It is basically a visual inspection and some 
functional/detective tests. If, in doing so, an adequate 
assessment is not possible, a disassembly of crane parts 
has to be undertaken to get access to the hidden parts. 
Chapter 4 “The repair Instructions” will illustrate crane 
repair procedures in detail on components which need 
disassembly and disengagement. 
The cautions and instructions about inspection and 
maintenance are outlined, aiming at giving a general idea 
on how to carry out an inspection and a routine 
maintenance.
