Examination may be according to following steps:  
1) 
Move the trolley to the parking position. Hoist 
and lower the spreader at slow speed for 
several times. Make the spreader as close to the 
ground surface as possible when lowering it. 
Examiner can stay on both sides of the main 
hoist drum to check the wire ropes. This step 
aims to check the wire ropes wrapped on the 
drum. Pay close attention to the moving drum      
while inspecting. When the spreader is lowered 
to the ground surface, rings on the drum shall 
be checked.  
2) 
Examiner can stand on the platform outside of 
sheave. Hoist and lower the spreader at slow 
speed for several times. Make the spreader as 
high as possible when hoisting it. This step 
aims to check the wire ropes of main working 
parts. Pay close attention to the moving sheave 
while inspecting. 
3) 
Lowering the spreader as low as possible, 
examiner can stand at the headblock to check 
the wire ropes nearby. 
4) 
The problematic area should be checked 
carefully.
