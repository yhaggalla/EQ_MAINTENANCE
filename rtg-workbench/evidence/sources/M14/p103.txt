Preparations before replacement: 
Jack up the crane with no suspended load in calm weather 
with spreader on ground. 
Lock out the crane’s motion which affect safety by 
performing lock-out and tag-out procedure. 
Enclose the safety area with guardrails, keeping away 
independent person. 
Move the trolley to the following position, to max travel 
opposite to the side of wheel need replacement: 
 
If the E-room and power house are at 
different sides, when jack up power house 
side wheels, trolley stop at max travel of 
E-room side; when jack up E-room side 
wheels, trolley stop at max travel of power 
house side. 
 
When power house and E-room are at same 
sides, when jack up power house side wheels, 
trolley stop at max travel of the other side, 
when jack up the other side wheels, trolley 
stop at max travel of power house side.
