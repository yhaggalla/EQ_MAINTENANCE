6.1.3 surface treatment 
1) All surfaces to be coated shall be clean of all 
contaminants that would affect the integrity of the 
coating system. Prior to surface preparation, the 
surfaces shall be cleaned, as necessary, to remove all 
oil, grease, dirt, salts or other foreign materials. This 
cleaning shall be done with appropriate detergent 
and/or solvent cleaning. Detergent or solvent cleaning 
shall be performed as outlined in SSPC-SP-1. 
2) SSPC-SP-3 。When approved, power tool 
cleaning shall be as per SSPC-SP-3 and SSPC-SP-11.  
Hand tool cleaning shall be as per SSPC-SP-2. 
3) 50mm around the damaged part of the painting 
need to be thinned to ensure the adhesive force for the 
original painting and the new painting. 
6.1.4 painting application 
1）Surface need to be clean, dry and dust free 
before apply ZP842 
2）Apply ZP842 within 4 hours after the surface 
treatment. 
3 ）Coatings shall be applied under the 
environmental conditions prescribed in the pertinent 
ZPMC’s product data sheets. 
4 ）Coat shall be applied as per ZPMC’s 
recommendations. 
5）Thickness requirements shall be met with each 
coat and total thickness shall not be “made-up” in any 
one coat. 
6）Maximum recoating interval time shall be as 
per ZPMC’s recommendations. 
7）All areas around the corners, welding lines, 
sharp edges, bolts and nuts should be applied with 
shop primer before painting.
