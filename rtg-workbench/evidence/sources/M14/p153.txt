5.6.2  Re-adjusting rope lengths 
During reeving of the wire ropes, the lengths of the 8 
ropes must be the same. If not, the stress of the 8 ropes 
will vary greatly and the spreader slewing position will be 
not correct. Re-adjusting rope lengths in 8-rope system is a 
complicated problem and needs to study carefully. The 
manpower and tools needed for the rope adjustment are 
given in Table 5-11.

 The procedure of the 8-rope adjustment is as follows: 
1) 
Ensure that the RTG is in Maintenance Mode. 
All other operations affecting the safety are 
banned. 
2) 
Rotate the spreader to zero position. If having 
tilting or translation function, the spreader also 
needs to return to zero position. 
2) 
Move the trolley to the parking position. Lower the 
spreader until it is 200~500mm to the ground to make 
sure it is convenient to observe. 
3) 
Observe whether the rotating beam is in a level. 
If not, adjust it by the adjusting screw. If the 
rotating beam is in a level, the spreader has been 
adjusted. Refer to Figure 5-38.
