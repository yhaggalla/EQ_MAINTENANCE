The next is a example of adjusting wheel: 
 
Figure4-148 example of adjusting wheel 
horizontal deviation 
This figure illustrates that the left wheel at the front of 
trolley have a inner horizontal deviation when the trolley 
run towards front. The left horizontal trolley wheel will 
press the left surface of trolley rail. The following method 
could be adopted to fix the problem. 
1) 
At the trolley horizontal wheel side if trolley 
wheel has a small inner horizontal deviation, then 
turn the outer eccentric sleeve of the trolley wheel 
clockwise an angle. 
2) 
If the wheel deviation is large, turn the outer 
eccentric sleeve of the wheel at the other side 
without horizontal trolley wheel. Turn the sleeve 
an angle clockwise, and observe the running 
condition. 
3) 
If the running condition gets better after adjusting 
turn the eccentric sleeves at same direction. If not, 
turn the eccentric sleeves at reverse direction. 
4) 
Sometimes, both two wheels have inner horizontal 
deviation is a better condition than two parallel 
wheels just like the toe-in of car structure. This 
arrangement make the trolley runs along a straight 
line more easily. 
5) 
Normally only outer eccentric sleeves need 
adjusting. 
6) 
Normally, only one wheel needs adjusting at a 
time, sometimes only two wheels need adjusting. 
Only in special condition all 4 wheels need 
adjusting. 
7) 
Clear up all tools. Have a test run of trolley, 
inspect the result. 
 
 
Appling the method to RTG 
trolley wheel properly may 
need a lot attempt before 
doing well.
