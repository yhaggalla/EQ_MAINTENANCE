4.9.3 Adjusting of horizontal trolley wheel 
A new RTG’s trolley wheel will run deviation after being 
used for a while, which   may be also seen after changing 
wheel. At this time adjusting for horizontal trolley wheel is 
needed.The adjusting of horizontal wheel is mainly to 
assist trolley wheel adjusting. The aim is to make trolley 
run along straight line, with even clearance at both sides 
between rail and horizontal wheels.While trolley running, 
only one side contacting of horizontal trolley wheel. 
Eccentric distance of wheel shaft is 5 mm. Shown in 
Figure 4-206. Adjusting clearance by rotating eccentric 
shaft.   
 
Figure4-206 eccentric structure of horizontal 
trolley wheel 
 
Adjusting procedure: 
Loose bolts which fix wheel shaft, create clearance 
between locating block and base. So, horizontal wheel can 
rotate freely. 
 
 
Hold shaft head with spanner, then turn it as below method 
to proper position. 
A） Turn spanner as red arrow direction, the 
clearance between wheel and rail gets 
smaller. 
 
Figure4-208 step 2 
B） Turn spanner as red arrow direction, the 
clearance between wheel and rail gets larger. 
 
Figure4-209 step3 
Then fasten the bolts of locating block.
