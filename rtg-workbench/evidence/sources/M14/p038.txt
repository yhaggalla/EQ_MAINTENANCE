2.4.10 Auxiliary facilities 
 
 2.4.10.1 Side guide rollers 
In order to ensure the trolley moving along the rail 
safely, two ends of one trolley beam install with a pair 
of guide rollers.  
Each set of side guide roller consists of a bracket, 
two eccentric shaft, two rollers and bearings etc. The 
gap between the guide roller and side of rail can be 
adjusted by eccentric shaft. The side guide rollers as 
shown in figure 2-48.  
 
 
 
 2.4.10.2 Trolley anchor device 
In order to prevent the trolley from moving by high winds when the RTG not working in a long time, 
two anchor devices have been set in both side of the trolley frame. When the trolley pariking in the anchor 
position, and the wind speed exceed the warning, the archor pin need to plug. When the pin plug, the limit 
switch will forbid the crane to be operated. If the trolley is not anchored and storm wind come it’ll be very 
dangerous. Even if it has the brake, the trolley could move along the rail. Once the trolley moving, it'll be 
enormous kinetic energy and may cause the accident
