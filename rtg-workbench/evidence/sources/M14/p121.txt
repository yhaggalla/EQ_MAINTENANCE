4.9 Replacement and adjusting of horizontal 
trolley wheel 
To avoid  rail gnawing, trolley wheel normally has no rim. 
Horizontal trolley wheel take over the leading function. 
Each horizontal trolley wheel is composed by 1 bracket, 2 
eccentric shafts, 2 wheels and bearings and etc. They are 
fixed to trolley frame by bolts. Assembly drawing is shown 
in Figure 4-194 & Figure 4-195.
