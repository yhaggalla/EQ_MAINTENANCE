4.7.3 Adjusting of trolley wheel 
To reduce self-weight, RTG frame normally is designed to 
be light and relatively soft. A new RTG’s trolley wheel will 
run deviation after being used for a while, which   may 
be also seen after changing wheel. This time adjusting for 
wheel is needed.
