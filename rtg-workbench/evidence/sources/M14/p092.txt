4.3.2 Replacement procedure 
Lower the spreader to the ground and slacken the Main 
Hoist ropes. If the trolley traveling motor coupling is 
replaced, the trolley need to stop at anchor position. If the 
gantry traveling motor coupling is replaced, the crane need 
to stop at the maintenance position, wheel is stopped by 
chocks. 
Lock out the crane’s motions which affect the safety by 
performing lock-out and tag-out procedure. 
Remove the limit switch device at drum end. 
To avoid the force on drum caused by self weight of wire 
rope, use chain block to tie down the wire ropes on reliable 
item on the trolley. 
Remove the electrical connection from the motor brake, 
release the brake manually, then remove the brake by 
service crane.
