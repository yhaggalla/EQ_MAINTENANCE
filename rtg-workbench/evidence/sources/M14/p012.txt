2.1.2  The main features of this project 
 
This equipment is specifically designed, manufactured and installated container handling 
mechineryaccording the contract for CICT, Sri Lanka, totally 40 rubber-tyre gantry cranes (hereinafter 
referred to as RTG) 
 
The RTG is the latest model for handling container. The design and manufacture is based on the 
long-term experience of ZPMC in crane manufacturing field and the latest technology. Compared with 
traditional RTG, it has advanced performance of pratical and efficient operation.
