5.6.1   Procedure of main hoist rope re-reeving in 
8-rope reeving system 
The 8-rope reeving system is shown in Figure 
5-36. 
 
Figure 0-36  8-rope reeving system 
Procedure of rope re-reeving is as follows: 
1) 
First of all, prepare some new wire ropes 
according to the length and quantity in the 
drawings. One end of the pressed joints should 
be done if pressed joints are used. Prepare a few 
hemp ropes whose diameter is about 20mm and 
steel wire for binding. 
1) 
Move the trolley to the parking position.  
2) 
Ensure that the RTG is in Maintenance Mode. 
All other operations affecting the safety are 
banned. 
3) 
Rotate the spreader to zero position. If having 
tilting or translation function, the spreader also 
needs to return to zero position. 
4) 
Lower the spreader to the ground and slacken 
the main hoist ropes until they are on the 
spreader headblock. Continue to slacken the 
ropes until about 1 loop to the securing bolts 
of rope clamps. After the ropes relax, close 
the operation system and diesel generator 
sets. 
5) 
Tie the 1 or 2~4 old ropes temporarily with 
hemp ropes or chain hoist (To prevent the ropes 
slipping off). 
6) 
Loosen the securing bolts of rope clamps on 
drum and take off the rope clamps. Then take 
off the rope from the drum. Bind the hemp rope 
with the end of the rope with mild steel wire. 
3~4 parts need to be bound, each part should be 
bound 10 loops and about 15cm apart. Connect 
the old rope end with the hemp rope by rope 
connecting 
device 
(Refer 
to 
Figure 
5-21).
 
 
Figure 0-21  Rope connecting device 
7) 
Tighten the hemp rope, lower the old rope to the 
ground slowly. Pay attention to observe the 
speed of the rope and whether the rope wears or 
hooks the structure. And whether the rope runs 
out of the sheave should be observed. On the 
headblock, if the rope wears the structure, 
rubbers, wood plates, and wear-resistant blocks 
should be fixed. 
8) 
Bind the hemp rope with the end of the old rope, 
take off the pin connecting the end of the rope 
(Usually called pressed joints) and the adjusting 
screw rod according to Figure 5-37. When 
taking off the pin, the end of the rope should be 
pulled (To prevent the rope end touching the 
headblock 
paint).
