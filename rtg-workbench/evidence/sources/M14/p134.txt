5.4 wire rope inspection 
Wire rope service life is limited and influences by 
many factors such as it quality, overload, environment, 
and the maintenance. Different damage types will occur 
as signs before it is useless. So regular inspection need to 
be taken to find these signs and hidden dangers and 
analysis the main reason to lead to the useless. Chang the 
wire rope before it is useless and eliminate the adverse 
factors in the future use to extend the service life.

5.4.1 factors influence the service life of wire rope 
wire rope service life influence by many factors, 
include the working condition, maintenance, working 
environment and ect. For RTG wire rope, the main 
factors are listed as follows: 
1) 
Wire rope bear variational load constantly and 
at the same time it need to pass through drums 
and sheaves, which make the wire rope 
buckling repeatedly, and is easy to cause the 
wire rope’s fatigue. 
2) 
Water, salt atmosphere make the wire rope 
corrosion: the moisture mixed the soluble salt, 
dissolved saline ions will accelerate the 
diffusion of the oxygen ions in the air, which 
result in the wire rope with the air oxidation 
reaction speed fold increase. 
3) 
The impact of the embedded foreign matter in 
the wire rope: because terminal operations are 
out of doors, hostile working environment and 
with big wind. Some objects such as grits, 
sands are easy to be involved in the sheaves, 
drums, wire ropes and even embedded in the 
center of the wire ropes which will cause local 
stress concentration and result in abnormal 
wear and surface wire broken. 
4) 
The impact of wire rope frequently operation: 
lubrication coat the wire as thin film. When the 
wire rope be tightened, some of the lubrication 
will be extruded out to the surface of the rope, 
while some will be absorbed back to interior of 
the rope. Repeated extrude out and absorb back 
will lead to the release of the interior 
lubrication and accelerate the oxidation of the 
lubrication.

rope service life, not only factors that involve in the 
design and usage, but also other abnormal factors, such as

Multi-layers of the rope on the drum can result
