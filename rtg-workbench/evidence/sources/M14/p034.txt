2.4.6 Operator’s cabin 
In order to provide the best view for the driver, operator’s cabin is positioned under the trolley frame, and 
connect the trolley with shock absorber. Left side of the operator’s cabin install the energy chain or festoon 
system. The trolley will drive the cabin and cable together. The driver in the cabin can observe spreader’s 
loading conditions. Arrangement of the operator’s cabin shown as in figure 2-35.
