4.5.2 Replacement procedure 
Lower the spreader to the ground and slacken the Main 
Hoist ropes. 
Avoid the crane’s motions which affect safety by 
performing lock-out and tag-out procedure. 
Only  necessary，remove the whole brake., next we will 
introduce how to replace the whole brake and its 
accessories.

and release the brake by hand.
