Lift the drum up slightly by lift or jack, make the bearing

Remove bearing block. Use crane or service crane, or 
chain block by temporary staging to lift the bearing block 
upright a little and move it transversely slowly. If the fit is 
tight, use puller to get the bearing block disconnected. 
Puller could be fixed to the bolt holes of base, then put 
jack between drum shaft and puller to get the puller move. 
Finally take the bearing and cover out. 
Inspect oil seals. Oil seals should be replaced if necessary. 
Install a new bearing. 
Re-assemble the bearing seat together in the reverse order 
mentioned above. Before reassembling, wash the drum 
shaft and bearing block with specified detergent and dry 
them with cool wind. 
Realign the drum couplings. Refer to related criteria. 
Verify the drum-mounted rotary limit switch setting and 
alignment. 
Remove lock-out and tag-out devices and Pre-Op the crane. 
Startup and monitor the crane to verify its operation before 
returning it to service.
