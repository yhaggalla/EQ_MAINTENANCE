5.3.2 wire rope cutting 
Before cutting the wire rope, the two end should be 
lashed with small- gauge wire, which can prevent the 
loosening of the wire rope. After lashing, the wire rope 
can be cut. Usually, each cutting end with one small- 
gauge wire is enough, but for counter- rotation wire rope, 
at least two small- gauge wire should be used, and at 6 
times the rope diameter apart. The procedures are as 
follows: 
1) 
Reel the small- gauge wire on the wire rope, 
and keep reeling parallel, closely and tightly. 
2) 
Using the pliers to make it tightly. 
3) 
After reeling one wire strand long, using the 
pliers to tighten the two ends and twist to knot. 
Gently knock the knot end to make it cling to 
the wire rope. 
4) 
After finishing one end, do the same thing at 
another end. And using the cutting machine to 
cut the wire rope after finish both.
