8) 
Compare the distance from the two closer 
corners of the spreader to the gantry. If the left 
one is 50mm larger than the right one, adjust as 
follows: 
A) Tighten up the left front screw rod for

C) Slacken the right front screw rod for 1/4

D) Tighten up the left back screw rod for

9) 
Compare the distance from the four corners of 
the spreader to the ground. If the left one is 
30mm less than the right one, adjust as follows: 
A) Tighten up the left front screw rod for 
1/2 loops. 
B) Tighten up the left back screw rod for 
1/2 loops. 
C) Slacken the right front screw rod for 1/2 
loops. 
D) Slacken the right back screw rod for 1/2 
loops. 
10) Compare the distance from the four corners of 
the spreader to the ground. If the front one is 
20mm larger than the back one, adjust as 
follows: 
A) Tighten up the left front screw rod for 
1/2 loops. 
B) Tighten up the right front screw rod for 
1/2 loops. 
C) Slacken the left back screw rod for 1/2 
loops. 
D) Slacken the right back screw rod for 1/2 
loops. 
11) Hoist and lower the spreader for several times 
and observe whether the beam is in a level. The 
adjustment may need many times to complete. 
Usually the distance from left to right shall be 
less than 30mm and the height difference shall be 
less than 20mm. The goal of the adjustment is to 
make it convenient for the driver to aim the box. 
12) Observe the difference of the four weight sensors 
in electrical room. And the difference shall be 
controlled within 30%. 
13) Various factors shall be considered due to the 
difference in all directions. The values above are 
for reference only. The adjustment may need 
many times to complete. 
14) Finally try to hoist and lower the heavy box for 
several times. Repeat the steps above until 
controlling the difference within specified range. 
15) Put the locking bolts into the screw rods, tighten 
the nuts and pins. Now the ropes have been 
adjusted.
