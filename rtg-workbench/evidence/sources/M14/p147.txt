5.5 Wire rope lubrication 
 
It has been proven that a well 
lubricated wire rope will last 
four times as many working 
hours as a non-lubricated 
wire rope.   
 
Always use wire rope 
manufacturer’s recommended 
lubricant! 
5.5.1 Importance of lubrication 
The importance of periodic lubrication will never be 
overstressed. The effects are as follows: 
1) 
Each time a rope bends or straightens, the 
wires in the strands of the rope must slide on 
each other. Thus, a film of lubrication should 
be present on each moving part. When exposed 
to the same test condition, a well-greased rope 
showed four times as many working cycles as 
a non-greased rope. 
2) 
A second important reason for lubricating the 
wire rope is that it keeps away moisture which 
can 
cause 
interior 
corrosion 
under 
simultaneous bacterial proliferation in the fiber 
core. The lubricant also prevents deterioration 
of the fiber core. A rusty rope is a liability, 
since there is no means of visual inspection, 
which can determine the remaining strength of 
a corroded rope. 
5.5.2 Selection and requirement of lubricant 
The main lubricants for wire ropes used in a 
container terminal are as below:  
1) 
Grease: Mainly is lithium soap base grease. 
Sometimes are mixed with mineral oil for 
better penetrability.  
2) 
Special lubricant for rope such as : ESSO 
SK(semi-liquid), FUCHS Ropelife (Grease), 
Yizhi601(Oil).
