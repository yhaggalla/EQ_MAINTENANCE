4.2.2 Replacement procedure 
To avoid the force on drum caused by self weight of wire 
rope, use chain block to tie down the wire ropes on reliable 
item such as post of Machinery Room and so on. 
Lower the spreader to the ground and slacken the Main 
Hoist ropes. 
Lock out the crane’s motion which affect safety by 
performing lock-out and tag-out procedure. 
Disconnect the limit switch device at the drum end. 
Remove the oil collecting plate at the other side. 
To avoid the force on drum caused by self weight of wire 
rope, use chain block to tie down the wire ropes on reliable 
item on the trolley. Slacken enough rope, so that the drum 
can be moved uprightly. 
Lift the side of bearing block with crane or RTG’s service 
crane. The method as below also for consideration: put a 
wood or bracket under the bearing block side of drum then 
use a 5t jack to support drum. For safety, add woods as the 
drum is jacked up, and tuck wedge block at both sides of
