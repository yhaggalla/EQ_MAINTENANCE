7.1 general 
Metal structure is the frame of the crane, and affects the performance and safety of the crane directly. The crane 
need to be shut down and carefully inspected, when crack is founded. If the crack is not find and repair in time, it 
will lead to structural damage which will not only broken the crane, but also cause the causalities. So structure needs 
the routine inspection and periodic annual inspection. 
 
The deformation of the structure part may be caused by the misuse of the crane，crack extension or bolt 
loosening. Inspection procedures and the routine inspections in the manual are needed to strictly obey. 
 
 
No attachment or device can be welded on to crane, without 
the written authorization of the responsible structural 
engineer. This is the crane’s structural integrity requirement.
 
The inspection ways of the structure include the follows: 
1) 
using the visual sense and experience to observe and judge the structure 
 
If the connecting part of the structure is stable, flexible and without abnormal noise when operation 
process. 
 
If there is any loosening of the bolts. 
 
If there is any paint wrinkle, peel off or weld crack phenomenon around the connecting part or welds. 
 
If there is any entire or local deformation, and any bend, fold, twist, damage phenomenon. 
2) 
Using the nondestructive inspection to do the periodic inspection of important welds. 
3) 
Using electrical logging tension method to do the carefully quantitative inspection of the metal structure, 
which can make a scientific judge to the metal structure’s status. 
the follows need to be pay more attention 
1) 
Inspect the structure deformation, corrosion, flaw, weld crack, bolt loosening in routine inspection, month 
and annual periodic inspection.  
2) 
Structure deformation will increase the concentric error of the mechanism which installed on it, increase 
the operation noise, lead to the bad mesh of the gears. 
3) 
Structure deformation, flaw extension, weld crack, bolt loosening should be report to the maintenance 
department instantly, and do the repair work under the guide of the structural engineer. 
4) 
The jam drainage holes need to be cleaned to avoid the corrosion of the structure. Repaint the rusty place.
