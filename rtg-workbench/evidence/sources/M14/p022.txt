2.4.1Gantry frame 
Shown as in Figure 2-3. Gantry frame is the main structure of the RTG, it consists of two girders, four legs 
and two sill beams. Most of the structure member section is box section except some in tubular shape. The brace 
bar on the upper legs connects the structure as a whole frame. The trolley rails are arranged on the girders, we 
used I-shaped steel or square bar as the rails. The rails will be fixed on the girder by rail clips or welding. The 
buffer support at the end of girder can also be used as lifting ear for the RTG.
