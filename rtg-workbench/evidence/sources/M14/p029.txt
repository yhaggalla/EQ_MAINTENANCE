2.4.4.4 Load sensor 
The crane is equipped with four load sensors. These load sensors connect to the four wire ropes of the main 
hoist reeving device, they measure the tension of each wire rope separately, and allow the PLC and CMS 
calculate and record the load and load eccentricity. The model of load sensors is shown in table 2-3. Basic 
element of weighing system comprises four sensors and an amplifier. Each load sensor will detect tension of one 
rope. Four tension signals come into analog signal by amplifier. After conversion the analog output signal to the 
digital meter in operator cabin and display the load in tons.  
 
The crane monitor the load sensor though the PLC. Under normal operation conditions, the system will 
identify the size of lifting rigging connected to the spreader. Programmable logic controller calculates the 
allowable eccentrically load according to the size of lifting rigging. If over load, it’ll stop the hoisting and over 
load alarm sound out from PA. At this time, the crane could only lower the goods in limit speed.

drum shaft, one of them is overspeed protection switch, 
another one is cam limit switch(as shown in Figure 2-22). 
The overspeed protection switch will be activated, when the 
speed reach 115% of normal speed. The switch will close the 
power of motor, dead stop all brakes. The cam limit switch 
controls the position of spreader. It’s the backup switch of
