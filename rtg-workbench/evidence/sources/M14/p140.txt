5.4.5.14 Local increase in diameter of rope  
See picture 17 in 错误！未找到引用源。. This 
condition usually relates to a deformation of the core 
such as in particular environments, a fiber core can swell 
up owing to the effect of moisture. If this condition 
causes the actual rope diameter to increase by 5 % or 
more, the rope shall be immediately discarded.  
5.4.5.15 Flattened portions  
See picture 19 in 错误！未找到引用源。. Flattened 
portions of rope which pass through a sheave will quickly 
deteriorate and may damage the sheave. In such cases the 
rope shall be discarded immediately. Flattened portions of 
rope in standing rigging can be exposed to accelerated 
corrosion, and shall be subject to inspection at a 
prescribed shortened frequency if retained in service.  
5.4.5.16 Kinks or tightened loops  
See picture 18 in 错误！未找到引用源。. Kink or 
tightened will cause imbalance of lay length and 
reduction in strength which will cause excessive wear, 
and in severe cases the rope will be so distorted that it 
will have only a small proportion of its strength 
remaining. Rope with a kink or tightened loop shall be 
immediately discarded.  
5.4.5.17 Bending  
Bends are angular deformations of the rope caused 
by external influence. Rope with a severe bend will suffer 
similarly to flattened portions of rope and should be 
treated as in Clause 错误！未找到引用源。.  
5.4.5.18 Damage due to heat or electric arcing  
Ropes that have been subjected to heat or electric 
arcing effects, externally recognized by the colours 
produced in the rope, shall be immediately discarded.
