3.5.5.3 The steps of sampling 
1) Take off the cover of a clean, dry sampling bottle and 
screw the bottle into the pump. 
 
Figure 3-112:  Step 1 
2) Shear the ends of a new section of pipe to 45 degree. 
 
Figure 3-22: Step 2 
3) Tuck the pipe in the pump until the pipe into the bottle 
through the top of the pump, and then tweak the screw 
to fix the pipe. 
 
Figure 3-12:  Step 3 
4) Tuck the end of the pipe into the sampling point, keep 
the bottle upward. Absorb the lubricant to the bottle 
with the pump, and remain 25mm air clearance. 
 
Figure 3-13:  Step 4 
5) Turn off the bottle and tweak the cover to avoid 
pollution. 
6) Fill in the sampling label clearly and stick it to the 
sampling bottle. The collection of lubricant is 
completed.
