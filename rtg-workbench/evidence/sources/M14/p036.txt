the interlayer is filled with insulation material. The normal temperature of the room control between 20℃
（68℉）and 30℃（86℉）through two air conditionings.  
 
The temperature switch install in the room setting at 30℃（86℉）. If the temperature over this value, it’ll be 
recorded and warning the operator through a flash signal. This fault will cause the hosit and trolley operated in 
slow speed.  
 
2.4.9 Energy chain system 
The trolley use the cable energh 
chain or festoon system to provide the 
power.  
 
Energy chain system as shown in 
Figure 2-43. The cable chain running 
smoothly in the stainless steel guide, with 
less influenced by the wind.              
 
This project use IGUS’s energy 
chain, type is 5050RHD.30.300.0. The chain connects the 
trolley with L bracket. The bracket should  avoid interfere 
with girder’s platform. As shown in Figure 2-44.  
 
Because of both trolley and energy chain has the 
installation and operation error, so we use floating 
connection between them. Shown as in Figure 2-45.
