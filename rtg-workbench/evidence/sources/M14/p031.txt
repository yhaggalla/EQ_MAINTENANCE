2.4.5 Trolley travel sysytem 
The trolley travel system consists of driving device and wheel supporting device, is mainly responsible 
for the horizontal transport of the container. Four non-flange wheels support the trolley frame, and driving 
device make the trolley moving along the rail. Because of the trolley wheels are non-flange, we have two sets of 
side guide rollers in both ends of one side trolley frame, and four groove shaped blocks under end beam of 
trolley, to avoid the trolley derailment.  
 
2.4.5.1 Trolley driving device 
Trolley driving device composed of motor, coupling, reducer and transmission device. According to the 
driving principle, it can be divided into wheel direct driving and rack and pinion driving. Wheel direct driving is 
using the friction between the wheel and rail to driving the trolley moving. Rack and pinion driving is using the 
mesh between the pinion and rack to driving the trolley. When the trolley is driving by wheel direct driving, the 
friction between the driving wheel and rail shall be big enough to avoid trolley skid.  
A)  Wheel direct driving device composed of motor, coupling, brake, reducer and universal drive shaft 
connect with two trolley wheels. There are two types, one is single wheel direct driving and another is dual 
wheels direct driving. Single wheel direct driving refers to Figure.2-24, dual wheels direct driving refers to
