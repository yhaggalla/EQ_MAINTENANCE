2.3 General introduce for RTG’s configuration 
RTG is one kind of port equipment used to handle container between yard block and truck. The below 
is RTG’s main components. The general arrangement is as shown in Figure 2-1. The trolley arrangement is 
shown in Figure 2-2. 
○
A Gantry frame: The main bodies are box section members. It’s consist by girder, leg and sill 
beam. 
○
B Gantry travelling mechanism: The RTG is supported by 8 rubber tyres. Motor drive and power 
transferred by reducer. It’s including the driving wheel system and driven wheel system.
