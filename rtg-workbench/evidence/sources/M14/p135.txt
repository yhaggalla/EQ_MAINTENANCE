5.4.2 wire rope inspection method 
 The wire rope inspection must be done by the 
person who is trained and authorized. 
1) 
wire rope diameter measurement 
The size of a wire rope is designated by its diameter. 
The diameter of a wire rope should be measured with a 
set of vernier calipers. 
The proper method is showed in figure 5-15(a), and 
5-15(b) is the improper method. 
To ensure an accurate measurement of the diameter 
of a wire rope, always measure the rope at three places 
and at least 1.5m apart. Use the average of the three 
measurements as the diameter of the rope.
