Common failures of rail support, rail clip 
and rail installations

1) Rail clip devices: 
a. 
Loosening of the bolts 
b. 
Breakage of bolts, studs, or weld fillets

1) 
Excessive wear or flattening of the rail head 
2) 
Wear of edges of rail head 
3) 
Rail breakage 
4) 
Deformation at joints caused by wheels running 
over gaps 
3.6.6.2.1Causes of dilapidation 
Figure 3-17 shows the acting force of the trolley. 
The crane operation causes repeated wheel loading of 
the rail resulting in fatiguing cycles. Fatigue failure 
occurs due to: 
①   High stress in the rail support,  
②  Rail movements causing fatigue in the rail 
attachment components.
