Chapter 7  Structural inspection and repair 
 
 
 
Summary 
 
Metal structure is the frame of the crane, and affects the performance and 
safety of the crane directly. Cracks need to be found and repair in time. To 
keep the crane always in good condition and ensure crane’s performance and 
safety, routine inspection and periodic annual inspection are needed. 
 
This chapter describes the procedures of the structural routine inspection 
and periodic annual inspection, and lists the inspection recording table.
