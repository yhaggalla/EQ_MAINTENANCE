1) 
Hoist over height stop 
Hammer limit switch is located under the over height 
position. This switch is connected with emergency stop 
circuit, when this limit switch activated, it’ll be emergency 
stop. The "limit bypass" selection switch located in the 
electrical room can release the limit state. The hammer limit 
switch shown as in Fig 2-23.  
 
2) 
Hoist normal stop 
Programmable software limit switch. When it activated, 
the switch will start ramp stop.  
 
3) 
Hoist smart slow down 
Programmable software limit switch (fuction). The position 
of this smart slow down is depending on the setting of hoist 
slow down. 
 More detail definition about “smart slow 
down” please refer to the electrical manual.  
 
4) 
Lowering smart slow down 
Programmable software limit switch (fuction). The position of this smart slow down is depending on the 
setting of lowering slow down.  
 
5) 
Lowering normal end stop 
Programmable software limit switch (fuction). When it activated, the switch will start ramp stop.  
 
6) 
Hoist stop check 
Cam limit switch. If the encoder hasn’t stop the hoisting speed, then activate this switch will stop the hoist 
speed.  
 
7) 
Hoist slow down check 
Cam limit switch. If the encoder hasn’t decelerate the hoisting speed, then activate this switch will 
decelerate the hoist speed.  
 
8) 
Lowering slow down check 
Cam limit switch. If the encoder hasn’t decelerate the lowering speed, then activate this switch will 
decelerate the lowering speed.  
 
9) 
Lowering terminal inspect 
Cam limit switch. It’s the mechanical fuction backup of the over lowering protection.
