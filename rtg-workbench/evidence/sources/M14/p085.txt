4.1 General 
4.1.1 Notice 
This chapter includes main components of RTG such as：
bearing block、motor coupling、drum coupling、motor 
brake、drum brake、trolley wheel、gantry wheel、main 
hoist sheave、wire rope and so on. 
The personnel who carry out the replacement work must 
be acquainted with the structure of components & the 
operation step and have the ability of coping with the 
abrupt problem. Before working, it is essential that set 
down a processing procedure in detail according to this 
chapter, have a pre-work meeting, prepare safety 
precautions，point someone to take responsible for safe 
coordination, and strictly obey the safety rule request  in 
chapter 1. 
 
Danger! Be care of stumbling! 
Keep away from hanging weight
Watch your hand!
