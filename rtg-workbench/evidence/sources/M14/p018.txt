8
○
C Trolley: Trolley is the main component of the RTG. It’s consist by four steel wheels and trolley frame. 
There is main hoist mechanism, trolley travelling mechanism, driver cabin (including control device) 
and kinds of auxiliary devices installed on the trolley. The reeving system is using to hang headblock 
and spreader. The driver in cabin can control RTG to handle stock and carry container. 
○
D Main hoist mechanism: The main hoist mechanism is located on the trolley. It’s used to lift spreader 
to hang container. 
○
E Trolley travelling mechanism: The trolley travelling mechanism is located on the trolley. It’s used to 
move the trolley to the required position. The trolley is self drive type. 
○
F Main hoist wire rope reeving system: The wire rope through sheave and connect the hoist drum and 
headblock. It could transfer relative movement between drum and headblock. 
○
G  Spreader skewing mechanism: It is achieved by two sets of electric push rod fixed on the 
headblock. 
○
H Spreader anti-sway system: The ansiway function is achieved by 8 hoist rope system, no 
other auxiliary antisway system. 
○
I Trolley’s walkway and platform: provided on trolley for maintenance equipments on the 
trolley. 
○
J Cabin: Driver can operate RTG and spreader in it. 
○
K Trolley anchor device: Two sets of manual operation pin are located on both sides of the trolley for 
locking the trolley, preventing accidentally moved 
   
○
L Service crane: None 
○
M Cable chain system: Cable chain system transport the power and control signal to main hoist 
mechanism, trolley travelling mechanism, spreader skewing mechanism, headblock and spreader. 
○
N Power unit: It provides power for RTG all motion mechanism and auxiliary equipment. It locates on 
the sill beam.  In this project, it is consisted by diesel generator + lithium battery system. 
○
O Walkway, ladder and platform: Including all the others platforms, handrails, stairs, 
ladders except fixed on the trolley. It is used for boarding the crane, and providing 
the maintaince space.
