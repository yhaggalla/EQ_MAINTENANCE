Chapter 1  Safety 
 
Summary 
 
 
Safety is a priority either in operation or in maintenance and 
inspection. This chapter defines the caution terms and definitions 
throughout the whole manual and informs you of the possible hazards and 
necessary precautions to be taken when operating or maintaining the crane. 
All the maintenance personal should read, understand and obey the 
safety rules described in this chapter before operating or maintaining 
the crane.
