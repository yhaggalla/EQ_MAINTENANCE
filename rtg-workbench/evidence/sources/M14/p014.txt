Electrical room is equipped with air condition, to ensure proper working temperature. 
 
 
◇ Main hoist mechanism is mounted on the trolley. It works with constant power control 
and according to the lifting load automatically adjusts the lifting speed. It is equipped 
with overload protection device, over speed protection device, to ensure work safety 
and efficiency.                                                                                  
 
◇ Trolley travelling mechanism is mounted on the trolley. It works with AC frequency 
conversion control. The trolley is driven by the wheel directly driven method. 
 
◇ Gantry travelling mechanism works with AC frequency conversion control. In order to 
avoid collision between crane to crane, or crane to container, the crane is provided 
with a manual and automatic steering system, and anti - collision device. 
 
◇ In safety facilities, there is overload protection device, diesel engine over speed 
protection, high oil temperature and low oil pressure signal device, wind speed 
indicator, crane anti-collision device on four corners, emergency stop button and imit 
switch, signal in the mechanism. 
     
◇ There is lighting and signal apparatus on the crane for night operations, and alarm 
when gantry travelling. The driver can communicate with others with the phone, enlarger 
and radio. 
  
◇ Diesel generator set is arranged in the power room on sill beam. It’s consisted by 
CAT diesel engine and Zhejiang Zhongyuan generator. It is equipped with a lithium battery 
system, and the energy recovery system. The generator is brushless self-excitation type. 
The gen-set has the following characteristics. 
a. Strong impact resistance, suitable for RTG frequent starting conditions. The 
frequency and voltage fluctuation is very small. 
b. Reliable electronic speed governor in diesel engine, and has high water temperature, 
low oil pressure, over speed protection device. 
c. Enough standby power, so the engine has enough power and keeps good work condition 
even after wearing. 
 
◇ The hoist wire ropes are used to anti sway by eight rope reeving system.  
 
◇ The spreader has skew function to reduce position time.
