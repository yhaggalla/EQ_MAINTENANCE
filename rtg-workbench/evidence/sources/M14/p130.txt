5.2.2 unwind and wind 
Figure 5-6 and 5-7 show the proper/improper 
methods of unwinding a wire rope from a reel. 
Considerable care must be taken when unwinding a wire 
rope. Their operational performance is substantially 
influenced by the way they are handled. Bending or 
twisting wire rope can cause internal damage that will 
adversely affect the operational life of the wire rope. 
Avoid unwinding wire rope on the ground to protect the 
wire rope from debris. If there is no other option, make 
sure the ground is clean or covered with boards or plastic 
sheets.
