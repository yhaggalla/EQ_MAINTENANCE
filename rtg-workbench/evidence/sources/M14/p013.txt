The RTG’s type is: trolley double driving (direct driving wheel), double box section girder, rated load 
40.5 ton, span 23.47m, and hoist height 21.2m above ground. 
 
In the following are the RTG’s main features 
 
◇ The RTG will be worked on the costumer’s yard. It meets the site’s infrastructure 
condition and the local weather conditions. Design, manufacture, erecting and 
acceptance are according to international and domestic standards and rules. It could 
be operated and maintenance 24 hours every day in normal weather conditions. The 
electrical, electronic and mechanical device shall meet the requirements of the weather 
conditions and the work cycle. 
 
◇ The RTG’s main structure is composed of two groups of box section structures of door 
frame, supported by 8 inflatable rubber tires. The trolley is located on the girders’ 
rail. It could move following the girder’s direction to handle the containers between 
the legs. The driver cabin is fixed under the trolley. The driver can control the whole 
movements of the RTG. 
 
◇ The RTG has a electrical telescopic spreader to handle a container which weight is 
40.5tons. It could lift a 9’6” high container to pass six stacking 9’6” containers’ 
height. It also could cross width with six row 8’containers with the gap between the 
containers 400 mm, and a container truck lane. The trolley’s effective travel range 
is not less than 18.5m. 
 
◇ The electric spreader is hanged under the trolley by wire rope system. The spreader 
is supplied by BROMMA. It could telescopic to 20’ and 40’ positions. It also could 
lift 45’ or 48’ container (with the corner fittings at the 40 'position). The spreader 
system is composed by headblock and spreader body to achieve rapid replacement.  
 
◇ There are three main mechanisms, main hoist, trolley travelling and gantry travelling. 
The main hoist, trolley travelling could be separated or simultaneously operated with 
rated load. As well as the gantry travelling, the trolley travelling could be separated 
or simultaneously operated with rated load too. 
 
◇ The RTG could move along the normal lane as straight line. Each wheel can be rotated 
90 degrees, to form a right angle with the original way, in order to change operation 
block. It also could be 360 ° turning around.  
 
◇ Operation and control of the RTG are realized by the all digital AC variable frequency 
drives, PLC and CMS. The control function is reliable and effective. The main electrical 
control equipments are installed in the electrical room which is above the sill beam.
