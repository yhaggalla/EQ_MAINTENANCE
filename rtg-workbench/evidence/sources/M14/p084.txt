Chapter 4 Replacement of wearing parts 
 
 
 
Summary: 
 
 
The RTG is worn, fatigued, distorted, eroded and degenerated inevitably while being used. Proper 
operation and timely maintenance can ensure each parts of crane to achieve expected lift. So when the parts 
of crane achieve normal life or wear limitation, how to repair the crane reasonably is the very important 
aspect of modern RTGs management. 
 
This chapter will introduce the replacing methods of RTG’s main components elaborately by illustrating 
pictures after expected lives of which are achieved.
