Chapter 6  Application, repairement, and inspection  of painting
 
 
 
 
Summary 
 
 
Corrosion of metals exist everywhere and make great harm. There are 1/10 steel 
loss for rust or other kinds of corrosion every year according to statistics. 
 
RTG usually work outdoor beside the coast, with great salt atmospheric, 
sometimes even suffer with corrosivity cargo (for instance, beside the bulk- cargo 
dock). Because the exist of the corrosion, the structure anticorrosive painting 
lose its effect, which not only impact the outlook of the crane, but also make 
the metal construction corrosion and decrease it’s loading ability, even broken 
the structure. 
 
Consumer should do the structure routing care and maintainence, arrange the 
renew of the painting and recover the anticorrosive fuction of the damaged 
painting, while ZPMC make sure the painting have the suitable anticorrosive 
ability and lifetime.  
 
 
Consumer should reinforce the management of the anticorrosion, enlength the anticorrosive lifetime of the 
painting, and it has great meaning for the crane’s safety normal work, enlength the lifetime of the crane and reduce 
the maintenance charge of the crane.
