4）Tyre 
 
Tyre assembly procedure is just the reversal of the 
disassembly procedure as above description. 
 
Before assembly, check all the components, a new 
one should be replaced if any cracking, broken, 
serious abrasion & rust are found.(cracking, 
broken and rust could lead to worse performance, 
distorting and returning may not be used) 
 
In any condition don’t try to re-machine, forge, 
heat, even brazing. Should be replaced by a new 
one or well one with same brand, dimensions. 
 
Clean the rust, ash and foreign matter at tyre lip 
and rim groove. Clean the inner room of tyre. 
 
To the tyre which has air leakage or no enough 
pressure, if you doubt the possibility of broken of 
wheel component, please don’t charge again.(Air 
leakage and no enough pressure may cause wheel 
broken or part shifting)  
5）air charging 
 
While charging the tyre, knocking lock ring or rim 
is prohibited. 
 
Before air charging, make sure all the parts are 
installed rightly. 
 
Don’t charge the tyre if the installation is not 
finished. Put tyre to the fence or other containers. 
First charge the pressure to 5psi/0.35bar and 
inspect the installation of all parts, the condition of 
O shape seal. If there is any problem, should 
recharge the tyre. If the there is no problem at 
5psi/0.35bar, then charge tyre continuously. 
 
While charging tyre standing on it is prohibited, 
suggest using extended tube type indicator to let 
the personnel out from the ejecting area of the tyre. 
Install indicator or pressure adjustor at inlet valve, 
pre-set wanted pressure value. If the tyre is in 
fence or other containers, don’t lean on it (for feat 
the jumping out of tyres). 
 
Don’t perform welding or heating work around 
tyre.(The heat will cause pressure change, then 
lead to explosion, the storehouse could catch fire)

6）storage 
 
 
Figure 4-128 tyre storage 
 
Normally, no matter tyre is new or old (tube is 
taken down from rim and get cleaned), needed to 
be stored at cool & dry condition, should be laid 
uprightly. Being laid horizontally is prohibited, to 
make the clearance between lips is proper. Keep 
away external force for feat of distorting.  
 
The following conditions should be avoided 
 direct sunshine exposure 
 ozone 
 oil or other contamination 
  high temperature and dampness 
 
If the tyre is stored open air, cover by canvas. If 
the tyre is installed to some equipment , a bracket 
should be used to avoid the load to be directly laid 
on tyre. If there is no choice, then rotate the tyre 
monthly for fear of deformation.
