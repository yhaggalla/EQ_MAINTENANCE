2.4.5.3 Trolley limit switch 
In order to make the trolley travelling safely in the effective working aera, the limit switchs are setted in 
both sides of the trolley rail. The slow down limit switch can slow the trolley speed, terminal limit switch can 
stop the trolley and over travel limit switch can finally stop the trolley. Trolley limit switch arrangement refer 
to Figure 2-30.  
 
 
Figure 2-30 Trolley limit switch arrangement 
 
1) 
Forward over travel stop  
Mechanical limit switch, setting at the end of girder, and connect with emergency stop circuit. When 
this limit switch is activated, it’ll be emergency stop. The "limit bypass" selection switch located in 
the electrical room can relieve the limit state.  
 
2) 
Backward over travel stop 
Mechanical limit switch, setting at the end of girder, and connect with emergency stop circuit. When 
this limit switch is activated, it’ll be emergency stop. The "limit bypass" selection switch located in 
the electrical room can relieve the limit state.  
 
3) 
Forward normal stop 
Mechanical limit switch. When this limit switch is actived the trolley will stop, it’s normal stop, no 
need bypass.  
 
4) 
Backward normal stop 
Mechanical limit switch. When this limit switch is activated the trolley will stop, it’s normal stop, no 
need bypass.  
 
5) 
Forward slow down limit switch 
Proximate limit switch. Suppose the trolley pass the slow down limit switch as 100% speed then the 
trolley slow down, until 10% speed travelling
