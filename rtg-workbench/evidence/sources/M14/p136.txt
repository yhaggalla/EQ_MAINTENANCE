3) 
hand feeling method 
Hand feeling method is holding the wire rope, and 
makes the wire rope pass through the hand slowly to feel 
the flaw on the wire rope surface. But this way has 
dangers, which need to be carefully used. Any sharp on 
the surface of the wire rope make cause hurt on the hand. 
To avoid the hurt, can use two pieces of semicircle wood 
tube which has the same inner diameter to feel the heave 
when it passes through the half tube. When hand feels the 
wood tube slightly jump, then take away the hands. 
Another method is cover the wire rope with one layer 
cotton yam, not only surface dirt can be cleared, but also 
the cotton yam will be grappled away when any edge 
heave is pass through. 
4) 
（NDT） 
There are lots of instruments on the market, which 
use the NDT to inspect the surface and inner flaw of the 
wire rope. The advantage of these instruments is they can 
easily inspect the inner part of the wire rope and find the 
flaw which is not easy to find. And the growing trend can 
be told to the inspector through the computer analysis. 
But the reliability needs to be verified through the fact. 
Other information refers to the NDT instrument manual. 
NDT using electromagnetic 
means may be used to detect 
broken wires and loss in 
metallic area. This method 
complements the visual 
examination but does not 
replace it. 
5.4.3 inspection part and period 
The whole length of the wire rope needs to be 
inspected in principle, and the easy damage parts need to 
pay more attention. For instance, wire rope parts which 
pass through the sheaves frequently, dead end, rope 
clamp position, roll in and roll out drum and sheaves, 
pass through the equalized sheaves are easily be ignored, 
because these parts of the wire rope are still. But the 
experience is that these parts have flexural fatigue for the 
wire rope’s springing and swaying. Wire rope parts which 
have been covered by drum or sheave grooves should be 
pay more attention too. 
Ocular estimate must be done before everyday work, 
especially the connecting part and dead end part. 
Wire rope should be carefully checked within one 
week, after first usage, and inspected within one week, 
after wire break occur. 
Every mouth regular inspection should be done after 
the first inspection.
