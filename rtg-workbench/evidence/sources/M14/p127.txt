Chapter 5 wire rope 
 
 
 
Summary 
 
 
Wire rope is the easily worn-out component, and not include in the guarantee 
scope. 
 
Usually wire rope brand is decided by consumers according to their experience, and the 
new wire rope is also purchase by consumers. As one of the most important component, ZPMC 
has pay a lot of attention to the wire rope, so we make this chapter. 
 
This chapter describe the common knowledge, storage, transport method and the 
criteria of wire rope replacement; and the inspection, lubrication, adjustment and 
exchange wire rope are the key points of this chapter.
