1. General 
Rubber-tyred gantry crane is used for handling containers in stack yards and for 
general cargo handling. 
 
The Crane is supported by 8 (eight) rubber-tyres and powered with a 
diesel-alternator set and battery system which supplies power through silicon 
controlled rectifier (SCR) to every movement  drive.  The Crane may move freely 
in the yard without the restrictions of trailing cable or external power source. The 
Crane is equipped with a telescopic spreader which is able to handle 20’, 40’, 45’ 
standard containers. The Crane is provided with 8 wire system to reduce spreader 
sway and increase productivity. The Crane also has a spreader skewing device for 
easier spotting of the container. A 90 and 360°wheel turning system allows ease 
of movement. 
 
The Crane is six plus one and one over six type. 
 
The gantry frame of the Crane is box-section welded. The trolley rail track is put 
on the upper girder. The equalizer beam is connected to the sill beam through a 
hinged pin that is fixed with a keeper board. For each equalizer beam, there is two 
bogie trucks and two rubber tyres. 
 
Main hoist system is controlled by constant power which allows the automatically 
adjustment of the hoisting speed due to the load. The main hoist has an overload 
protection device and over-speed protection to ensure safe and efficient operation 
of the Crane. 
 
The trolley traversing is provided with AC control. An operator’s cab is put 
beneath the trolley frame. Operator will operate the Crane in the cab. Trolley is 
two-wheel driven type.  
The gantry traveling is also AC control system. To prevent the collision with other 
cranes and containers, auto-steering system and anti-collision system are 
provided. 
 
Protective system for the crane includes overload protection, diesel engine 
over-speed protection, high water temperature and low oil (lubrication)pressure 
signaling, anti-collision devices for gantry traveling located on four corner, 
emergency stop buttons, spreader load indicator, limit switch for every movement 
and signal indicators, etc. 
 
The Crane has lighting and signaling equipment for operation at night. Gantry 
traveling is provided with alarms. For intercommunication between the operator 
and the dock the crane has telephones, a public address system and radio 
speakerphone as well as Remote Control Management System.
