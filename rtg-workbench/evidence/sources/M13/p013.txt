6.2 Main hoist wire rope reeving system 
There are 8(eight) main hoist wire ropes (from the end of the reducer) of 43.5，
42.3m，42.3m，44.9m ,44.9m,42.3m,42.3m,43.5m and they are IWRC 6xWS36  B 
(right spiral) with diameter of 29 mm. 8 pieces of wire rope go from the drum and 
downward to headblock; If uneven tension happens, the end securing clamps may 
be loosened and tightened to adjust the wire rope. After the first period of crane 
working, the ropes must be re-adjusted depending on their stretch. In normal 
operation, any uneven tension situation happen between the eight sets of wire 
ropes, they should be corrected immediately. 
 
Attention shall frequently be paid to the fastening condition of the wire rope 
clamps and tighten them if loo-sened. Frequently check bolts of the clamps and 
replace them immediately if thread damage, cracks, etc. are found. The ropes shall 
not be twisted or knotted during reeving. 
 
The wire rope shall frequently be checked during application and shall be replaced 
if wires damaged account for 1/10 the wire in the whole strand or if the diameter 
of the wire rope is reduced by 7% of its nominal size due to surface wear. Pay 
attention to inspection for the rope jumping out of them sheave groove and if there 
is any interference of the wire rope with other mechanical or structural parts. 
 
6.3 Attention during Operation 
 
After a long term of non-operation of them crane or after disassembly and 
re-assembly, the crane shall be run first without load enabling the reducers, 
bearings, etc. to be lubricated sufficiently and operation with load can be started 
only it is confirmed that the running condition is normal.
