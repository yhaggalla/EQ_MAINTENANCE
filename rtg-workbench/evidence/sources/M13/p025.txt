12.6 In case of accident 
 
In case of any abnormal noise or accident , the spreader shall be removed from the 
headblock and inspected and repaired. 
 
12.7 Period Maintenance 
 
The spreader shall be carefully maintained since it directly lifts the load. 
 
12.7.1 Inspection before operation 
 
Refer to Points for attention in operation 
 
12.7.2 Inspection per month 
 
12.7.2.1 Visual inspection 
 
12.7.2.1.1 Examine the base frame, spreader beam joints, brackets, flippers, etc. for 
cracks. 
 
12.7.2.1.2 Any paint peeling off ? 
 
12.7.2.2 Any trouble in the twistlock device ? 
 
12.7.2.3 Is lubrication sufficient ? 
 
12.7.2.4 Any loosening of nuts, bolts and piping fittings ? Tighten if necessary. 
 
12.7.2.5 Is insulation of electrical parts correct ? 
 
12.7.2.6 Is there loosening of the screws for connection of the wires into junction 
box ? 
 
12.7.2.7 During hydraulic oil replacement that is once a year, the filter should be 
cleaned. 
 
12.7.2.8 Inspection per year 
 
In addition to the monthly inspection, the following shall be inspected once a year. 
 
12.7.2.8.1 The strength inspection, and the major location may be inspected 
through dyeing (including the base frame, spreader beam joints etc.)
