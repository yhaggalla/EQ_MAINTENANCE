6.4.5 Gearing coupling

  Period:  routine 
 
6.4.6 Drum 
 
6.4.6.1 Check for loosening of the securing bolts 
  Period:  routine 
 
6.4.6.2 Check whether wear of the rope grooves exceeds limit value which is 
generally 20% wire rope diameter 
  Period:  routine 
 
6.4.6.3 Check the lubrication of the wire role, grease regularly 
  Period:  once a month
