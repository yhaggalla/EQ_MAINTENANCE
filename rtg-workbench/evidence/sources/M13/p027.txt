13.2.9 Carefully check the wire rope end securing bolts, especially the following 
bolts and re-tighten them. 
 
13.2.9.1 Securing bolts on wire rope clamps on the drum. 
 
13.2.9.2 During wire rope replacement care must be taken not to damage the 
thread on the bolts for the rope clamp because with damaged thread the bolts can 
not be tightened to adequate torque. 
 
Also take care not to damage the threads on other bolts of the mechanical devices. 
 
13.2.10 After installation has been completed or after re-tightening, loosening of 
some bolts in mechanical parts may result in misalignment, vibration, heating, etc. 
That may cause reduction of service life of the corresponding components or may 
be the cause of running failure. 
 
13.2.11 Routinely re-tighten bolts, nuts, etc.
