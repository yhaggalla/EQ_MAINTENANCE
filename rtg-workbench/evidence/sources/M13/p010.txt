3.  Attention for Safety 
3.1 Preparation before crane starting 
 
3.1.1 Check for obstructions in the crane path.                        
 
3.1.2 Check that lubrication for every mechanical part is sufficient. 
 
3.1.3 Check that every operation joysticks and operation switch is in normal 
position . 
 
3.1.4 Check that power supply voltage is normal. 
 
3.1.5 A trial run without load should be done first and the running with load can 
be stared only after it is confirmed that various safety devices and limit switches 
are activated normally. 
 
3.2 Attention after operation 
 
3.2.1 Park the crane at the assigned location. 
 
3.2.2 Park the trolley at the stowed location, i.e., anchoring location. 
 
3.2.3 Return all the operation joysticks and switches in the cab to neutral position 
and specified position. 
 
3.2.4 Check the rubber-tyred wheels. 
3.2.5 If the crane is not expected to be in use for a long period or a storm is forecast, 
The crane should be secured with chock or anti-storm tie downs. 
 
3.2.6 Repair defects found during operation and confirm that all lubricants are 
correct for the next operation. 
 
3.2.7 Lock the doors of the operator’s cab and electrical room to prevent 
unauthorized entry. 
 
3.3 Attention for operator 
 
3.3.1 Operator should go up/down the RTG according the route regulated, i.e., 
through the stairs on the stair-side and then to the operator’s cab via the platform.  
 
3.3.2 The following should be paid attention while lifting the containers: lifted 
container should be 500 to 600 mm higher than the top of container stack.
