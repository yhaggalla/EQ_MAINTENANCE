10.2 Points for Attention in operation 
 
10.2.1 No heating equipment is supplied with this machine. 
 
10.2.2 Below 0C, antifreeze should be added into the cooling water to prevent 
breakage of the engine body result from the freezing. 
 
10.3 Inspection Items and period for maintenance 
 
10.3.1 Make a record of water temperature, oil pressure, etc. Daily. 
 
10.3.2 The air filter is provided with block indicator and the red color display in 
the indicator shows that the strainer is blocked by dust and should be replaced. 
 
10.3.3 The lubrication oil filter normally serves for 250 hours and then the filter 
element is replaced. 
 
10.3.4 The fuel filter serves 250 hours and the filter element is then replaced. 
 
10.3.5 Automatic louvers in power house shall be maintain and clean every six 
month. 
 
10.3.6 Maintenance of the engine and alternator shall be done in accordance with 
the Instructions for the diesel engine and the alternator. 
 
11.  Energy-chain Mechanism 
11.1 Construction and performance 
 
Control power of the main hoist, trolley traversing, spreader skewing and the 
power for the spreader is supplied through one row energy chain system. The 
energy chain is made by Germany IGUS product. One end of the chain is fixed to 
the girder, and the other end is fixed to the trolley, and the cable is led into the 
junction box on the operator’s cab. 
 
11.2 Points for Attention and Maintenance 
 
11.2.1 frequently check bolt connections and fastening by bolts and re-tighten bolts 
if they are loosened.
