6.  Main Hoist 
6.1 Drive device 
 
The drive mechanism of the main hoist is installed at the center part of the trolley 
and consists of an A.C. motor, a high speed flexible coupling, a gear reducer, a low 
speed teeth-type coupling which drives the wire rope drum, two high-speed 
brake， and tachometer, over-speed protection, limit protection, etc. The motor 
drives the reducer through a flexible coupling and drives the drum through the 
teeth-type coupling to wind up or pay out the eight set of wire ropes which in turn 
lift or lower the spreader. The disc brake is fitted on the reducer high speed shaft 
ends. The teeth-type coupling has a grease nipple fitting to grease it periodically 
and a drainage hole is at 180 degree opposite the fitting to discharge old grease. 
Wear of the teeth-type coupling should be inspected periodically. The brake disc 
should be replaced if it is worn to the value equal to 30% of its thickness. Each of 
the braking torque of the disc brake shall be 1.25 times greater than the motor 
output torque.  
 
The four stage cylindrical helical gear reducer has a centre-to-centre distance of 
1565 mm, and its high speed and low speed shafts are laid on the same horizontal 
plane. The reducer casing is the split type and split at the gear wheel’s centre. The 
split baying surface is sealed with sealing glue. An oil drainage valve and an oil 
level indicator with scales are on the lower casing underneath the low speed shaft 
to control the lubrication. A manhole hatch cover is on the lower casing at the high 
speed shaft end bottom for cleaning the inner portion of the reducer casing. The 
cover casing has an air breather and inspection hole. Two setting pins are provided
